Orion Assistant

The Orion Assistant in Express: the AI panel you open from the header, and the two places a result in it can take you. One rule runs through all of it — Orion drafts and builds, you approve and send. Nothing here sends anything, because that is the feature, not a limitation of the prototype.

  • My Workspace

    Start here. The Orion mark sits in the header, right of Command Launch, pulsing until you open it. Ask it something — or open “Browse Prompt Suggestions” and pick one. It answers from Express Help, reads your data, or tells you what to do next, depending on what you asked. Ask it to build a tile and the tile appears in its own column on this screen. The panel drags and resizes, and the conversation follows you to the other two screens and back.

  • Report viewer

    Where PDF on an analyzed result lands. A real paginated document per the Rent Manager Reports Guide, with thumbnails, zoom and a built (not borrowed) print dialog. “Analyze with Orion”, bottom right, reads the report back and can write its summary into the document. Normally reached from the assistant; this link opens the occupancy report directly.

  • Tenant detail

    Where a record card in a conversation opens to. Normally reached from the assistant; this link opens Marcus Reed directly.

The assistant travels with you: open a conversation on My Workspace, follow a result into a report or a tenant record, and the conversation is still there. See PROTOTYPE.md for what is faked, which icons are substitutions, and the deviations from RMX worth raising.
